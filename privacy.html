Privacy Policy

App: Calculator · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Calculator app ("the app") handles information. Short version: it collects nothing.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal data, no usage data, no device identifiers and no location data. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in.

2. Permissions

  • Vibrate – optional haptic feedback when you press a key.

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send information anywhere, even in principle.

3. Advertising and analytics

There are no advertisements, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters and no third-party tracking libraries in the app.

4. Data storage

All calculations happen in memory on your device. The app does not store your calculation history on disk or in the cloud.

5. Children's privacy

Because no data is collected from any user, no data is collected from children. The app is safe for all ages.

6. Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the Email developer link on our Google Play listing, or open an issue on GitHub.
